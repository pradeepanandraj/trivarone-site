About

Trivarone is one person doing the work properly.

The work is agency work. What is different is that one person does all of it, so there is no account manager between you and whoever is actually searching, writing and correcting.

The founder

Pradeep Anandraj

I spent eight years as an Oracle database administrator. That work taught me one thing that turned out to matter more than the technology itself: a business runs on how well its information is organised, and most of the difficulty people experience comes from information that is scattered, inconsistent, or missing entirely.

I later qualified in bookkeeping and accounting, and held a mortgage broker licence. That period put me on the other side of the desk from small business owners dealing with compliance, paperwork and administration — the parts of running a business that nobody starts a business to do.

Trivarone came out of both. Being found is an information problem before it is a marketing problem. A practice that is described inconsistently across its listings, its website and its profiles is difficult for a search engine to place and difficult for an AI system to recommend. Fixing that is patient, unglamorous work, and it is the work I do.

Most of my work is with practitioners in private practice and with NDIS providers. What I bring to it is not clinical knowledge — I have none, and I do not pretend otherwise. It is the habit of checking which rules apply before writing, and of telling a practice plainly when something is not worth doing.

How Trivarone works

Four commitments, kept on every engagement.

  1. 01
    The check comes before the recommendation

    Nothing is recommended before the practice has been searched. The findings decide the work, so no two engagements look the same and there is no standard package waiting to be sold.

  2. 02
    Regulator confirmed, never assumed

    Which advertising rules apply is established before a word is written. A physiotherapist, a dentist and a support coordinator answer to different bodies, and the restrictions differ. Getting it wrong creates exposure for the practice, not for Trivarone, so it is checked rather than guessed.

  3. 03
    Observed, not asserted

    Findings state which query was run, on which system, and on what date. AI answers vary between sessions, so a single screenshot proves very little. Where something is uncertain it is written as uncertain.

  4. 04
    What not to spend money on

    Every report says which findings are not worth acting on, and which causes cannot be bought at any price — how far you are from the person searching, and how long the practice has existed. It is usually the most useful section, and the one there is no commercial reason to write.
